Crouching now bends the hips and knees instead of squashing the character.
Your helmet, backpack and blaster keep their full proportions.

New movement: C crouches. Sprint with Shift + WASD, then tap C to slide.
Space jumps out of a slide; C brakes. Hold Space higher, release for a hop.
C again stands when there is room. Weapons and building work while crouched.

New: branching trees, real bark and rock textures, meadow grass and ferns.
Clear shallows reveal the seabed; light, wind and coastal reflections bring it alive.
Live FPS below the map, plus Smooth / Balanced / Detailed graphics.
Click the FPS badge or pause and choose Graphics & FPS.
Start with Balanced; try Smooth if play feels choppy. Aim for a steady 60 FPS.
Your quality choice and Show FPS preference save on this computer.
All presets keep the same cover, ramps, opponents, and rooftop crates.

1. Extract the ZIP if you are opening the portable edition.
2. Double-click "Launch Sunspark Rumble.cmd" on Windows.
   Or open "index.html" in Chrome, Edge, or Firefox on any desktop computer.
3. Choose Easy, Medium, Hard, or Insane and click LET'S RUMBLE!

Easy: active bots with forgiving aim and longer breaks between attacks.
Medium: flanking, cover, and occasional building.
Hard: sharper aim, climbing, and more building.
Insane: fast, relentless pressure. Keep moving and build cover!

Bots reload too: look for a cyan ring and a RELOADING label when aiming at one.
They can build walls, ramps, and floors using their own limited wood.

Try Practice playground first to learn without enemy attacks or a storm.
All four blasters are beside the practice spawn!
Walk near one, select slot 1 or 2, then press E to swap.

SPARK: steady golden fire for medium range.
CONFETTI: a wide party burst for close range.
PRISM: click to charge, then a rainbow shot fires automatically. Right mouse zooms.
GUMBALL: colorful balls bounce once. Try an angled shot off a wall!

WASD: move     Mouse: look     Left click: fire     Right click: aim
Shift: sprint     Space: jump     1/2: switch weapon     R: reload
B: enter/leave build mode     Esc/P: pause
While building: 1 wall / 2 ramp / 3 floor     R: rotate     Click: place
Q/E also cycle pieces. B brings your equipped blaster back.

Build a ramp, walk up, and place another to keep climbing.
Switch to floors to make a bridge. Look up to stack walls.
Green = ready. Red = blocked. SNAPPED = connected to an existing piece.

Aim at builds to see their health. HIT means your cover took damage.
BROKE! means the piece is gone and later shots can pass through.

Walk over pickups to collect them. Each build costs 10 wood.
Ammo boxes restock both carried guns. Weapons require E to swap.
Stay inside the white circle on the map. Last spark standing wins!

Adjust sensitivity, sound, or graphics in Controls & settings.
If mouse capture is blocked, hold right mouse and drag to look, or use arrow keys.

No accounts, internet connection, purchases, or installations are needed.
Keep all of the extracted files together in the same folder.
Read README.md for more details. Have a sunny little adventure!
