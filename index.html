C crouches; C again stands when there is room. Sprint, then tap C to slide. Space jumps out; C brakes. Walk over supplies to collect them. Press E near a weapon to swap your selected slot. Prism charges then fires automatically; Gumball bounces once. Builds snap together. Walk up ramps to extend higher, or look up to stack walls. Each piece costs 10 wood. Follow the map’s white circle to stay out of the purple storm.
